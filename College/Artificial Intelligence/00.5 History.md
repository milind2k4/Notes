# History and Applications

## History and Evolution

### Timeline of Intelligence

AI has gone through cycles of hype ("AI Summers") and stagnation ("AI Winters").

- **1950 - Turing Test:** Alan Turing proposes "Can machines think?" and the Turing Test.
- **1956 - Dartmouth Conference:** The term "Artificial Intelligence" is coined. Birth of AI as a field.
- **1966 - ELIZA:** First chatbot mimicking a psychotherapist.
- **1974-1980 - First AI Winter:** Funding dried up due to overpromising and underdelivering.
- **1980s - Expert Systems:** Boom in rule-based systems for specific domains (e.g., medical diagnosis).
- **1997 - Deep Blue:** IBM's Deep Blue beats Garry Kasparov at Chess.
- **2012 - AlexNet (Deep Learning Boom):** Neural networks crush image recognition benchmarks. GPU era begins.
- **2017 - Transformers:** "Attention Is All You Need" paper revolutionizes NLP.
- **2022+ - Generative AI:** ChatGPT, Stable Diffusion. AI creates content.

### Why Now?

Why did AI explode recently despite existing for decades?

1.  **Big Data:** We generate zettabytes of data to train models.
2.  **Hardware (GPUs):** Parallel processing power (NVIDIA) made training deep networks feasible.
3.  **Algorithms:** Improvements like Backpropagation, ReLU, and Transformers.
